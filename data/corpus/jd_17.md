# Senior Backend Engineer, Payments
Remote (United States)

## About Meridian
Meridian builds payment infrastructure for online marketplaces. We move money for about
four thousand sellers, and the team that does it is eleven engineers. We care a lot about
correctness, because the failure mode here is somebody's rent.

## What you'll do
- Own services in the payouts path, from ledger writes through to bank submission
- Design idempotent APIs that behave under retries and partial failure
- Work directly with our compliance team on reconciliation tooling
- Carry a share of the weekday support rotation with the rest of the team

## Requirements
- 5+ years building backend services in production
- Strong Python or Go
- Comfortable reasoning about distributed systems and eventual consistency
- Experience with relational databases and transactional correctness

## Nice to have
- Prior work in payments, ledgers, or double-entry accounting systems
- Familiarity with Temporal or similar workflow engines
- Experience with gRPC

## Benefits
- Health, dental, and vision coverage
- Four weeks of paid time off
- Annual learning and conference budget
- Home office equipment stipend
