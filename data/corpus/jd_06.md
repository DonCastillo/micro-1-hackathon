# iOS Engineer
Remote (United States)

## About Foldstar
Foldstar is a reading app with about two hundred thousand monthly users. The mobile team
is small, so you will own features end to end.

You will need to be based in Atlanta, GA within 90 days of joining the team.

## What you'll do
- Ship features in our Swift codebase
- Improve cold start time and scroll performance
- Build offline-first sync that survives bad connections
- Take part in code review across the mobile team

## Requirements
- 3+ years of iOS development
- Strong Swift, comfortable with SwiftUI and UIKit interop
- Experience with local persistence and sync
- Shipped apps you can point to

## Nice to have
- Experience with accessibility on iOS
- Familiarity with Swift Concurrency
- Interest in typography and reading experience

## Benefits
- Health, dental, and vision coverage
- Four weeks of paid time off
- Device budget
- Books, obviously
