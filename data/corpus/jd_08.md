# Full Stack Engineer
Remote (United States)

## About Salt & Stone
Salt & Stone is a direct-to-consumer home goods brand. The engineering team is six people
supporting a storefront that does most of its volume in two months of the year.

## What you'll do
- Build customer-facing features across our Next.js storefront and Node backend
- Improve checkout conversion through measurement, not guesswork
- Keep the site fast during seasonal traffic spikes
- Integrate with fulfillment and inventory partners

## Requirements
- 3+ years of full stack work
- Strong JavaScript or TypeScript on both sides of the wire
- Experience with a modern React framework
- Comfort with performance measurement
- We run a business-hours on-call rotation shared across the team, roughly one week per quarter.

## Nice to have
- Prior e-commerce or payments integration work
- Experience with headless CMS platforms
- Interest in web performance budgets

## Benefits
- Health, dental, and vision coverage
- Three weeks of paid time off
- Generous product discount
- Learning stipend
