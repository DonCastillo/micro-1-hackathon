# Integrations Engineer
Remote (United States)

## About Cartwright
Cartwright connects freight brokers to carrier systems. Most of the work is making other
people's APIs behave, which is less glamorous and more interesting than it sounds.

## What you'll do
- Build and maintain integrations with carrier and TMS partners
- Design resilient sync in the face of partner outages and schema drift
- Own the mapping layer that normalizes wildly inconsistent partner data
- Work with partner engineering teams to resolve upstream issues

## Requirements
- 3+ years of backend or integration engineering
- Strong Python or Java
- Experience with third-party API integration and its failure modes
- Patience for XML, EDI, and other formats nobody chose

## Nice to have
- Logistics or supply chain domain knowledge
- Experience with message queues
- Background in data mapping and transformation

## Benefits
- Health, dental, and vision coverage
- Three weeks of paid time off, plus company holidays
- Learning budget
- Equipment stipend
