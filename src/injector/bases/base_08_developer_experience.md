# Developer Experience Engineer
Remote (United States)

## About Ravelin
Ravelin sells an API for logistics routing. Our customers are developers, so developer
experience is the product, not a support function.

## What you'll do
- Own our client SDKs across three languages
- Write documentation that engineers can actually follow
- Build sample applications and integration guides
- Turn recurring support questions into product or documentation fixes

## Requirements
- 4+ years writing production software
- Strong technical writing, with samples you can share
- Comfortable across at least two of Python, TypeScript, and Ruby
- Genuine interest in the developer's first hour with a product

## Nice to have
- Prior SDK or API design experience
- Experience with OpenAPI tooling
- Conference speaking or technical blogging

## Benefits
- Health, dental, and vision coverage
- Flexible paid time off
- Learning budget
- Equipment of your choosing
